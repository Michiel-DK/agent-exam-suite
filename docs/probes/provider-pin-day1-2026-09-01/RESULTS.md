## The drift verdict — bounded to what two day-pairs can say

1. **No day-level drift episode occurred on THIS pair (1 Sep → 3 Sep):** cross-day
   verdict flips are AT OR BELOW the within-day bar for both arms and both models —
   GLM pinned 12/118 vs bar 14, unpinned 11/118 vs bar 19; Kimi pinned 7/118 and
   unpinned 8/118 vs bar 9. **Drift is episodic, not constant**: the 29→31 Aug episode
   (5–6 heldout cases beyond the bar) did not repeat. Consequence: pinning's
   drift-suppression effect is UNMEASURABLE on a calm pair — you cannot schedule a
   drift episode. Finding 8 is hereby bounded, not extended.
2. **But net pooled heldout is visibly steadier pinned, on both models, across all
   four measurement points** (day-1 rep1, rep2, day-2 rep3):
   - GLM pinned 54 → 55 → 54 (range 1) vs unpinned 55 → 49 → 50 (range 6)
   - Kimi pinned 55 → 53 → 55 (range 2) vs unpinned 58 → 55 → 54 (range 4)
   Flip-counts alone do not separate the arms; the net-score ranges do, in the same
   direction as day-1's weak stability signal. Probe-grade (one day-pair, one rep on
   day 2) — a direction, not a ranking.
3. **Pinning remains score-neutral** — no arm paid a capability price in any window.
4. **Mistral×crm: the pin does NOT rescue it** — pinned to Mistral's own creator
   endpoint, the trajectory burst still 429-gapped 3/3 (`.mistral_retry_done`).
   Contrast GLM, where the z-ai pin fixed the identical symptom on day 1. The 429
   family has (at least) two causes: marketplace routing (pin fixes it) and
   provider-side rate limits (pin cannot).
